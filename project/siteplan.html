Slam Poetry Club – Site Plan

Site Name

SlamVoices

The name SlamVoices was chosen to reflect the deep essence of slam Poetry: the power of the human voice as a way to express and share thoughts, emotion, a tool of expression and social commentary. "Slam" references to the art and "Voices" to the way of expression.

Optional domain: slamvoices.org

>

Site Purpose

SlamVoices is for slam poetry enthusiasts, beginners, and curious visitors. the site provide:

  • An introduction to slam poetry and its history
  • A clear explanation of slam competition rules
  • Profiles of notable slam poets and their works
  • A membership form for visitors who want to join a local slam club

Scenarios

The following questions represent what a typical visitor to SlamVoices might ask:

  • What is slam poetry and how is it different from traditional poetry?
  • What are the rules of a sam poetry competition and how can I participate?
  • How can I find or join a slam poetry club near me?
